ClearPath is a comprehensive digital platform for managing student clearance, exam eligibility verification, and academic workflow automation.

▶ Go to App 📖 API Documentation

Key Features

Everything you need to manage student clearance and exam eligibility in one place.

✅

Clearance Workflow

End-to-end digital clearance with multi-step approvals across departments, libraries, labs, and sports offices.

📝

Exam Eligibility

Automated eligibility verification ensures only cleared students can access exam halls and generate admission cards.

📄

Digital Certificates

Generate signed PDF clearance certificates with QR codes for instant verification by authorized parties.

📊

Analytics & Reports

Real-time dashboards with clearance statistics, trends, and exportable reports for administrators.

🔔

Notifications

Multi-channel notifications (email, in-app) keep students and officers informed of clearance status changes.

🔒

Role-Based Access

Granular permissions for students, officers, invigilators, and administrators with secure JWT authentication.

System Roles

ClearPath serves all stakeholders in the academic clearance ecosystem.

📚

Library Officer

🔬

Lab Officer

⚽

Sports Officer

Clear sports equipment and dues

🛠

Invigilator

🕵

Admin

Manage users, settings, and system configuration
